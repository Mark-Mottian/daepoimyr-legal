Pulse Support

Help with Pulse, the minimalist precision puzzle game published under the Daepoimyr brand.

Contact support

Email mark.mottian@gmail.com.

For a technical issue, you may voluntarily include:

  • your device model;
  • your Android version;
  • your Pulse version; and
  • what happened and the steps that led to it.

Pulse Circuit and purchases

Google Play handles payment for the one-time Pulse Circuit purchase. If legitimate ownership is not showing, use Restore Purchase in Pulse to refresh and reverify it.

Purchase, refund, or revocation matters may also involve Google Play. For Google Play purchase help, visit Google Play refund and purchase support.

Keep sensitive information private

Do not email passwords, payment-card information, raw purchase tokens, Integrity tokens, service-account credentials, private keys, or other private credentials.
